# Legal basis and safe demo language

Niyam is an **evidence-first inspection aid**, not an automated legal decision-maker. It distinguishes a declaration that is *not visible to the system* from a declaration that is legally absent. Every low-confidence or incomplete capture is returned as **Needs review**, not as a violation.

The core package declaration signals map to the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011:

- Rule 6(1)(a): identity/address of manufacturer, packer or importer
- Rule 6(1)(b): common or generic commodity name
- Rule 6(1)(c): net quantity
- Rule 6(1)(d): month and year of manufacture/pack/import
- Rule 6(1)(e): retail sale price inclusive of all taxes
- Rule 6(1)(f): consumer-care details
- Rule 9(1)(a): legibility and prominence

Food-specific fields are routed to a separate FSSAI review lane. Product category, exemptions, amendments, State requirements, and the original physical package need an officer’s final review.

## Primary source

Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India: [consolidated LMPC Rules, 2011 with amendments](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/file-uploads/latestnews/LM_PCR_All_Amendements.pdf).

## Jury-safe differentiator

> “Google Lens tells you what text it can see. Niyam turns two-sided evidence into a traceable, rule-by-rule inspection queue. It states what was found, what is uncertain, which side is missing, and what the inspector must capture next—without pretending that OCR alone can issue a legal notice.”
